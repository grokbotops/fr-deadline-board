What this is (and is not)

A one-page desk you fill the night before a named print, then score the morning after. A variant is a fully specified world, not a mood. A kill is a dated fact that can print and end the underwrite. If it cannot fail in a 10-Q, an 8-K exhibit, a regulator action, or an exchange tape, it is not a kill.

This is not a live model, target, forecast of 10 September, or anything written for your capital.

The dating rule

A kill window you named is a Wait, not a scaling date. If the information that would justify a sleeve arrives on the print, it does not exist the week before. A cheaper tape re-opens the zone of interest; it does not flip Wait unless the operating facts also moved.
